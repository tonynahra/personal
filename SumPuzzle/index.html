How to Play

  • Click a letter on the board to select it.
  • Click a number to guess its value.
  • Every letter represents a unique digit (0-9).
  • Solve the addition problem before time runs out!
  • Skip: Stuck? Get a new puzzle for a score cost.

Scoring System

  • Correct: +100 Base Points.
  • Speed Bonus: Faster = More points.
  • Penalty: Wrong guess reduces score (-25).
  • Hint/Skip: Costs 50 points.
  • Win/Fail: Affects difficulty level.
